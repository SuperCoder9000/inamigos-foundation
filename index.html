Who We Are
About InAmigos
Foundation
2020 Founded in Chhattisgarh

Founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO), InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization headquartered in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh. With operations spanning multiple Indian states including Delhi, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, and Madhya Pradesh, the foundation addresses critical societal challenges through a network of dedicated volunteers and corporate partners.


Our mission is simple: "Serve the community compassionately and create opportunities for every underprivileged person to grow with dignity and respect." We believe in the power of collective action — every contribution, big or small, drives real, measurable change.

Our Work
Flagship Projects & Initiatives

Six powerful programs — each designed to tackle a different root cause of inequality and suffering across India.

🍱

Project SEVA

Providing free food, clothing, and essential supplies to the underprivileged. Over 50,000+ meals have been distributed to those in need across multiple drives.

Food & Clothing
📚

Project BACHPANSHALA

Bridging educational gaps for underprivileged children through digital literacy training, life skills, and school education support — nurturing the leaders of tomorrow.

Education
💪

Project UDAAN

Empowering women through skill development, vocational training, and financial independence — helping them soar beyond barriers and build sustainable livelihoods.

Women Empowerment
🌿

Project PRAKRITI

Driving environmental conservation through tree planting drives, sustainability awareness, and community-led green initiatives to protect our planet for future generations.

Environment
🐾

Project JEEV

Dedicated to animal welfare — feeding stray animals, promoting compassionate treatment, and running drives to ensure our furry friends are cared for and protected.

Animal Welfare
🚀

Project VIKAS

Enhancing employability through skill development programs and internship training. Over 30,000+ interns have been trained across India in digital and professional skills.

Skill Development
Why It Matters
Social Impact &
Our Purpose
🍽️

Fighting Hunger

Millions in India go to bed hungry each night. Our feeding drives ensure consistent nutrition for homeless individuals, daily wage workers, and marginalized families.

📖

Right to Education

Education is the greatest equalizer. By bringing digital literacy and foundational learning to underprivileged children, we break the cycle of poverty from the root.

♀️

Gender Equality

When women are economically empowered, entire communities benefit. Project Udaan ensures women have the skills and confidence to build independent, dignified lives.

🌱

A Greener Tomorrow

Environmental degradation affects the poorest communities most. Project Prakriti drives sustainable change through local action and community awareness programs.

🤝

Youth Empowerment

India's greatest resource is its youth. Project Vikas channels the energy of young people into skill-building, professional growth, and social change leadership.

🐕

Compassion for All Life

Our circle of compassion extends to animals too. A society's character is measured by how it treats the vulnerable — and we include every living being in that definition.
